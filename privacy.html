Now Mode privacy

Your plans stay on this device. Nothing is collected.

English

Tasks, notes, wins, names, photos, sounds, and reminder choices are stored on the phone or browser you are using. Now Mode does not upload them.

There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising. Now Mode does not sell or share your plans.

Reminders are scheduled on the device. If announcement alerts are turned on later, they only carry app news. You can erase everything by clearing this app's data.

Español (Latinoamérica)

Tareas, notas, logros, nombres, fotos, sonidos y avisos se guardan en el teléfono o el navegador que estás usando. Now Mode no los sube.

No hay cuenta, ni analítica, ni publicidad. Now Mode no vende ni comparte tus planes. No se recolecta nada.

Los avisos se programan en el dispositivo. Si más adelante hay anuncios de la app, solo traen noticias de Now Mode. Puedes borrar todo limpiando los datos de esta app.
